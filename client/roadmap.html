Snapshot + migration portal

The $KAIROS snapshot time is announced in advance. Holders bind their Solana wallet to a k1 Kairos address.

  • Snapshot announced with a fixed date and time
  • Migration portal: connect Solana wallet, bind a k1 address
  • Genesis allocation file published for anyone to check
V
GENESIS
mainnet

Kairos mainnet.

Genesis is mined with every bound $KAIROS balance written in, one for one. From the first block on, new KAIROS only exists because a miner proved a block.

  • Genesis block with 1:1 migration allocations
  • Mining open to everyone, 100% of rewards to miners
  • Wallets, explorer and RPC pointed at mainnet

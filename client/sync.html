  • Estimates for October 2026, rounded. Bitcoin: full validation of the ~800 GB chain (most nodes skip old-signature checks with assumevalid, which is faster but trusts a hardcoded checkpoint). Ethereum: full snap-sync of an execution + consensus client (~1.4 TB), which trusts recent state from peers; replaying from genesis takes weeks. Solana: joining from a validator-provided snapshot (~120 GB); full ledger replay is not practical for individuals.
  • Kairos: download the live state, one ≈1 MB terminal proof and 18 headers, then verify the proof (about 1 s on a laptop in upstream measurements). State size scales with live outputs, not chain age; the default here is a young network's state.
  • Growth per year assumed: Bitcoin ~70 GB, Ethereum ~250 GB, Solana snapshot ~25 GB. Machine presets scale CPU-bound replay time.
